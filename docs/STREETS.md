## Weird Ideas

These feel unique enough that I don't immediately associate them with Earth:

### Thread

> Moon Thread
> Third Thread
> Eastern Thread

Makes the city feel woven together.

### Stroke

> First Stroke
> Moon Stroke
> Fifth Stroke

Feels artistic and calligraphic.

### Meridian

> Meridian Three
> Eastern Meridian

Elegant and astronomical.

### Span

> Crescent Span
> Market Span

Simple and believable.

### Trace

> Moon Trace
> Pilgrim Trace

Has a nice "old route worn into the landscape" feeling.

### Veil

> First Veil
> Outer Veil
> Crescent Veil

Particularly good for Bibi Shirif given the modesty themes.

If I were designing Bibi Shirif from scratch, I'd seriously consider a system where **major roads are "Meridians"**, **minor roads are "Threads"**, and **ring roads are "Veils."**

So a person might live at:

> 27 Third Thread, East Meridian District

which immediately feels like it belongs to a different civilization without sounding artificial.
